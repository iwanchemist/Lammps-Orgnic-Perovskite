Parameter ledger
=================

PEROVSKITE
CsPbI3 Buckingham-Coulomb parameters:
RSC Advances 2020, DOI 10.1039/D0RA08434D.
q(Cs)=+1.36, q(Pb)=+2.03, q(I)=-1.13.
A and C were converted from eV to kcal/mol for LAMMPS real units.

PHENOTHIAZINE
Neutral C12H9NS.
3D geometry: RDKit/MMFF optimization.
Atomic charges: RDKit Gasteiger charges; total charge ~0.
PTZ is modeled as one rigid body.

IMPORTANT
The PTZ LJ self terms are OPLS-like starting values.
The inorganic LJ values used to generate PTZ-inorganic cross terms are
screening placeholders, not literature-validated CsPbI3/PTZ parameters.
Do not interpret adsorption energies from this starter system as quantitative
until those cross terms are validated against DFT adsorption-energy/distance scans.
